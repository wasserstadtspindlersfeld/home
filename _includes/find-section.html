{%- comment -%} Finds the section (from _data/sections.yml) the current page belongs to, by its first folder, so pages inside a section (like the heating calculator) get the section's colour too. Sets `section` and `section_n` (its number in the menu) for the layout that includes it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign section = nil -%} {%- assign section_n = nil -%} {%- assign here = page.url | split: '/' -%} {%- for s in site.data.sections -%} {%- assign there = s.url | split: '/' -%} {%- if s.url == page.url or there[1] == here[1] -%} {%- assign section = s -%} {%- assign section_n = forloop.index -%} {%- break -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
