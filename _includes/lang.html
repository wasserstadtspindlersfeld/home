{%- comment -%} Works out the page's language. Sets `lang` ("en" or "de"), `t` (its words from _data/i18n.yml), `lp` (the address prefix of its pages: "" or "/de"), `path` (the page's address without that prefix) and `other_url` (the same page in the other language). German pages live under de/. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = page.lang | default: site.lang -%} {%- assign t = site.data.i18n[lang] | default: site.data.i18n.en -%} {%- if lang == 'de' -%} {%- assign lp = '/de' -%} {%- assign path = page.url | remove_first: '/de' -%} {%- assign other_url = path -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign lp = '' -%} {%- assign path = page.url -%} {%- assign other_url = '/de' | append: page.url -%} {%- endif -%}
